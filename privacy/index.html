Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026 · Last updated: October 2, 2026

OddsBuddy keeps your watchlist on your iPhone. The developer doesn't collect your personal data, run analytics, or track you.

The short version

  • Your watchlist, notes, and alerts are stored only on your device.
  • No account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.
  • To show live prices, your device requests public market data directly from Polymarket.
  • Apple handles subscriptions and payments. OddsBuddy never sees your payment details.

Data stored on your device

Your followed markets, cached prices and price history, notes, and alerts are saved in OddsBuddy's local storage on your iPhone. This data isn't sent to the developer, and OddsBuddy has no accounts or cloud sync. Device backups (iCloud or computer) may include it, depending on your Apple settings. Deleting the app deletes this data from your device.

Data the developer collects

None. OddsBuddy has no developer-operated server and includes no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking software. The developer can't see what you search for, which markets you follow, or what you write.

Requests to Polymarket

When you search for markets, paste a market link, or refresh prices, your device sends the search text and market identifiers directly to Polymarket's public data services (gamma-api.polymarket.com and clob.polymarket.com) to fetch the information you asked for. Like any website or service you connect to, Polymarket receives standard connection information such as your IP address. Polymarket handles those requests under its own privacy policy. OddsBuddy is not affiliated with Polymarket.

Subscriptions and payments

If you subscribe to OddsBuddy Pro, Apple processes the purchase and payment. OddsBuddy receives only the subscription status from Apple's StoreKit on your device, so it can unlock Pro features. It never receives your name, email, or payment card details. Apple handles purchase data under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Permissions

OddsBuddy doesn't request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or notifications, and it doesn't send push notifications.

Children

OddsBuddy is not directed to children, and it doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If OddsBuddy's data practices change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.
